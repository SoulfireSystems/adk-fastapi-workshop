# 017 - Soulfire Studios Film & TV

## First assignment
Take this existing Soulfire concept and develop one concrete screen package: format, logline, audience, story engine, central characters, pilot or feature beat structure, one finished opening scene and a realistic production-scale brief. This is a proposed behavioral test, not a result from a live evaluation.
