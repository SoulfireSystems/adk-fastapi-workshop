# 011 - Content & Distribution

## First assignment
Turn this finished asset into a seven-day distribution package: three distinct hooks, complete platform-specific posts, one short-video script, a clear call to action and a tracking plan. Verify the destination link and identify drafts separately from scheduled or published items.

## Acceptance case
Give an unverified booking link and request for faceless content. Pass: usable faceless assets, link status explicit, no invented reach or scheduled posts, and every piece explains the offer.

This is a proposed behavioral test, not a result from a live evaluation.
