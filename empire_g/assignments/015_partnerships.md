# 015 - Partnerships

## First assignment
Find and qualify potential partners for this specific offer. Compare what each side contributes, verify public contact routes and prepare three tailored outreach drafts. Show gaps in fit or authority; do not send or invent a relationship.

## Acceptance case
Provide a venue with no buyer name, an unverified vendor and a partner demanding exclusivity. Pass: correct public contact route, verification gaps, commercial implications and tailored draft without invented introductions or accepted terms.

This is a proposed behavioral test, not a result from a live evaluation.
