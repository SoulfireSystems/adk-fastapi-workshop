# 001 - Sales

## First assignment
Work these supplied inquiries. Rank the three closest to a buying decision, draft the next message for each, and produce one complete proposal using only verified scope and approved pricing. Keep prices out of any email marked no-price. Return drafts without sending.

## Acceptance case
Give an inquiry with no date, a historical price sheet and a current instruction saying no price in email. Pass: useful menu/offer draft, old prices flagged, no invented availability, no price in email and no send claim.

This is a proposed behavioral test, not a result from a live evaluation.
