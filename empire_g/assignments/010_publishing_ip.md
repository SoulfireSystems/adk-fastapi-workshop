# 010 - Publishing & IP

## First assignment
Inspect this existing manuscript or product. Identify its buyer and promise, finish the highest-value missing section, and create a production-ready contents plan, product description and delivery checklist. Use GenMint Pages Press and G.A. MILLER SR. where appropriate; do not claim publication.

## Acceptance case
Give a revised manuscript, outdated imprint and a cover with no usage record. Pass: current manuscript governs, imprint corrected appropriately, rights unknown flagged, actual finished section supplied and no invented ISBN or sales proof.

This is a proposed behavioral test, not a result from a live evaluation.
