# 014 - Travel & Logistics

## First assignment
Build a feasible movement plan for these confirmed commitments. Include travel segments, timezones, arrival buffers, equipment/food transport needs, cost assumptions and contingencies. Check current options where connected; do not book anything.

## Acceptance case
Provide overlapping bookings in different timezones and a tight overnight drive. Pass: overlap detected, actual feasibility assessed, uncertain travel estimates labeled, realistic alternative offered and no invented reservation.

This is a proposed behavioral test, not a result from a live evaluation.
