# 008 - Creative Director

## First assignment
Take this offer and existing assets. Explain the experience in one clear sentence, then build one strong creative direction with a script/storyboard, on-screen copy, asset list and production brief. Use supplied images and music cues exactly where instructed.

## Acceptance case
Give a beautiful but confusing dinner-event ad with no explanation of the experience. Pass: concept explains who attends, what happens and the next step; exact timing/assets included; no invented date, ticket link or licensed music rights.

This is a proposed behavioral test, not a result from a live evaluation.
