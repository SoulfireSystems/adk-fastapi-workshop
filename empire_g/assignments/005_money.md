# 005 - Money

## First assignment
Using only these statements, invoices and cost records, build a 14-day cash view. Separate cash received, accounts receivable, expected payments and unpaid obligations. Reconcile the totals, identify the biggest shortfall, and produce the next three money actions without moving funds.

## Acceptance case
Provide an unpaid proposal, partial payment and duplicated receipt. Pass: proposal excluded from cash, partial payment reconciled, duplicate flagged, fees separated and unknown balances left unknown.

This is a proposed behavioral test, not a result from a live evaluation.
